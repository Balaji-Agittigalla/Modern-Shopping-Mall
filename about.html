Our Story

A Legacy Built on Craftsmanship, Trust, and Vision Since 1947

From a Single Sewing Machine to a Shopping Landmark

In 1947, when India was witnessing the dawn of a new era, Sri A. Narayanaswamy embarked on a journey of his own. With nothing more than a single sewing machine, exceptional tailoring skills, and an unwavering commitment to quality, he opened a small tailoring shop in a rented premises on SJ Road, now known as MG Road, Kolar.

What began as a humble one-man operation soon earned the trust and admiration of customers across the region. His dedication to perfection transformed Modern Tailors into one of the most respected tailoring establishments in Kolar.

Known especially for its expertly crafted suits, the business grew steadily from a single machine to a thriving workshop with skilled craftsmen, laying the foundation for a legacy that would span generations.

OUR LEGACY

Journey of Modern Shopping Mall

Located on Mahatma Gandhi Road, Gowripete, Kolar, Modern Shopping Mall represents nearly eight decades of growth, innovation, and customer trust—serving generations through tailoring, textiles, fashion, and modern retail experiences.

1947 The Beginning
🏬

The Beginning

Sri A. Narayanaswamy establishes Modern Tailors in a rented shop on SJ Road (now MG Road), Kolar, with a single sewing machine.

1953 First Milestone
🏠

First Milestone

Purchases the first owned building through personal savings, creating a permanent foundation for the business.

1950s Growth
🧵

Growth of Modern Tailors

Modern Tailors grows from 1 machine to 5 machines and 5 skilled tailors.

Late 1950s Excellence
✂️

Tailoring Excellence

Expands to 25 sewing machines, 25 craftsmen, and expert cutting masters, becoming Kolar's most renowned destination for custom-tailored suits.

1960 Emporium
🏪

Modern Cloth Emporium

Tailoring operations transition into textile retail and Modern Tailoring Hall becomes Modern Cloth Emporium.

1960 Textiles
🧶

Textile Expansion

Expansion into sarees, shirting materials, suiting fabrics, and textile retail.

1960s Manufacturing
🏭

Manufacturing Begins

Contract manufacturing of sarees begins.

1970 Expansion
👔

Retail Expansion

School uniforms and ready-made garments are introduced.

1970 Next Gen
👨‍👩‍👧

Next Generation Joins

1998 Foundation
🏗️

Future Foundation

Development of an owned commercial building strengthens the foundation for future expansion.

2000 Leadership
✨
